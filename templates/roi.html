Each investor's share of the overall gain/loss is allocated proportionally to their net contribution (deposits minus withdrawals) as a fraction of total net contributions. This is a simple capital-weighted split — it does not time-weight contributions, so capital added later shares equally (by %) in gains/losses that accrued before it was invested. Manage expectations accordingly for contributions made at very different times.
