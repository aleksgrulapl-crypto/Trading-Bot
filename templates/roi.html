Each investor's gain/loss is computed with NAV-per-unit accounting: a deposit buys units of the fund at the current NAV, and a withdrawal redeems units at the current NAV. Every closed trade's PnL moves the NAV for everyone holding units at that moment — so an investor only shares in gains or losses from trades closed after their own deposit, never retroactively. {% if breakdown.owner_name %} {{ breakdown.owner_name }} additionally receives a discretionary operator allocation of {{ "%.0f"|format(breakdown.owner_override_pct) }}% for covering backend/hosting costs, on profitable closed TradingView/Hedge trades only.
