This is a manual bookkeeping ledger only — entries here do not move real money. Record actual deposits/withdrawals you make directly with your broker, and log broker fees (e.g. Capital.com commission/financing charges) here so they stay out of the trade log/analytics as noise but are still reflected in the ROI page, deducted from the Overall P&L. A Withdrawal with operator Backend covers backend costs: the same percentage of units is taken from every investor. A Backend Deposit adds units back the same way (to balance an over-estimated cost). If the cost did not come out of the fund balance (you paid it personally), use Backend (Owner paid): investors lose units pro-rata and those units are credited to the owner.
